# Amazon India Executive Management Dashboard

The **Amazon India Executive Management Dashboard** is an Excel-based data analytics project designed to provide a clear and comprehensive overview of business performance.

The dashboard helps management monitor key business metrics and analyze sales, profitability, product performance, customer order status, payment methods, fulfilment methods, and geographic performance through interactive charts, visualizations, and Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).

### Key Features
- **Overall Sales & Profit Analysis:** Total sales, total profit, total orders, units sold, average order value, and profit margin.
- **Category & Product Performance:** Category-wise sales, profit, units sold, and Top 10 products by sales.
- **Order Status Analysis:** Delivered, shipped, returned, and cancelled orders, including return and cancellation rates.
- **Payment & Fulfilment Analysis:** Sales and profit by payment method and fulfilment method.
- **Geographic Performance:** State-wise sales, profit, and order analysis.
- **Monthly Trends:** Monthly sales and profit performance.

### Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Excel
- Data Cleaning
- Data Analysis
- Pivot Tables
- Excel Formulas
- Charts & Data Visualization
- KPI Reporting

### Project Objective
To transform raw Amazon India sales data into meaningful business insights through an easy-to-understand executive dashboard, helping management monitor performance and make informed business decisions.
